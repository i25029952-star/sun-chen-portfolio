Explore Highlights

Shaolin Temple

The cradle of Zen Buddhism and Chinese Kung Fu, attracting martial arts enthusiasts worldwide since 495 AD.

Henan Museum

Housing over 170,000 cultural relics spanning 5,000 years of Chinese history and civilization.

Henan Opera

One of China's most influential regional operas, celebrated for its passionate singing and vivid storytelling traditions.

Mount Song

One of the Five Great Mountains of China, rising 1,500 meters with ancient temples and stunning natural scenery.

Yellow River

China's "Mother River" flows through Zhengzhou's northern outskirts, offering breathtaking panoramic views.

Urban Parks

Zhengzhou's green spaces and botanical gardens provide peaceful retreats within the bustling city landscape.

CBD District

Zhengdong New Area showcases the city's modern face with towering skyscrapers and innovative architecture.

Railway Hub

Asia's largest passenger railway station connects Zhengzhou to every major Chinese city within hours.

Night Markets

Experience Zhengzhou's vibrant nightlife through its famous Erqi night markets and bustling entertainment districts.

Experience the Heart of China

Zhengzhou invites you to walk through millennia of history, taste the flavors of authentic Henan cuisine, and witness a city that bridges ancient wisdom with modern ambition.
