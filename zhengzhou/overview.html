City Overview

The Heart of Central China — A millenniums-old metropolis on the Yellow River with 3,600 years of history and a population of over 12 million.

Discover Zhengzhou

A Millenniums-Old Metropolis

Zhengzhou, the capital of Henan Province, is a vibrant city that seamlessly blends ancient heritage with modern urbanization. Located in the heart of the Central Plains, it serves as a crucial transportation hub connecting China's major regions. As one of the Eight Great Ancient Capitals of China, Zhengzhou boasts a history spanning over 3,600 years, serving as the political, economic, and cultural center during the Shang Dynasty.

Modern Transformation

Today, with a population exceeding 12 million, Zhengzhou has transformed into a modern metropolis while preserving its profound historical legacy. The city is home to cutting-edge infrastructure, booming industries, and a thriving cultural scene that celebrates both its ancient past and forward-looking future.

Historical Timeline

1500 BC

Shang Dynasty Capital

Zhengzhou served as a major capital during the Shang Dynasty, marked by the discovery of ancient city walls and bronze artifacts revealing a sophisticated urban civilization.

495 AD

Shaolin Temple Founded

The legendary Shaolin Temple was established at the foot of Mount Song, becoming the birthplace of Zen Buddhism and Chinese martial arts.

1904

Railway Era Begins

The completion of the Jinghan Railway transformed Zhengzhou into China's most important railway junction, igniting rapid industrial and commercial growth.

1954

Provincial Capital

Zhengzhou was officially designated as the capital of Henan Province, accelerating its development into a major administrative and economic center.

2013

Global Connectivity

The launch of the China-Europe Railway Express established Zhengzhou as a pivotal node in the Belt and Road Initiative, connecting to European markets.
